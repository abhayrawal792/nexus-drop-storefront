# Storefront QA Notes

The public routes for Home, Shop, category collections, Cart, Product Detail, and Delivery Policy render successfully on desktop and mobile. Mobile navigation, customer-facing page hierarchy, and the new Cart route are visible and responsive.

The category collection view was refined to resolve the selected category to its database identifier before querying products. The dedicated Jewelry, Watches, Eyewear, and Bags routes now receive only category-matched catalog data.

The customer site now has separate Home, Shop, category collection, Cart, Checkout, Product Detail, Account, Order Confirmation, and policy routes. Desktop and mobile screenshots confirmed that the routes render, while the next QA pass will confirm the full click-by-click customer journey and remaining payment asset details.
